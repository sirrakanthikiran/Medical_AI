From hospital data to AI prediction

Three interactive demos illustrating how HL7 FHIR enables transparent, reproducible AI pipelines in healthcare — based on Namli et al. (2024).

1 Pipeline overview Diagram

The five stages from raw EHR data to AI prediction, tracing one fictional patient (Ravi, post cardiac surgery).
2 Ravi's walkthrough Interactive

Click through five tabs to see what Ravi's data actually looks like at each stage — raw EHR, FHIR resource, JSON recipe, clean feature table, and final AI prediction.
3 How FHIR standardizes hospital data Diagram

The same blood pressure reading from three hospitals (India, USA, Spain) converges into one FHIR Observation. Includes the four pillars of FHIR standardization.
Source paper: Namli T, Sınacı AA, Gönül S, et al. A scalable and transparent data pipeline for AI-enabled health data ecosystems. Frontiers in Medicine. 2024;11:1393123. doi:10.3389/fmed.2024.1393123
